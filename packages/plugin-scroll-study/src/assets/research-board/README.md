# Research board artwork

Source reference: `../bg_white_edges.png`. Both images were separated using the built-in image_gen tool and converted to WebP for the plugin.

- `frame.webp`: transparent pixel-art wooden frame with meander corners and lotus ornaments, lossless 1254 × 1254. Rendered as one proportional texture over the square research board, without sliced corners or repeating rails.
- `parchment.webp`: pale cream paper texture without wooden borders, used only by the research board as a proportional background.

Frame prompt: Extract only the dark wooden pixel-art frame; retain original corners, lotus ornaments, brown colors and rail thickness; remove all cream paper to alpha transparency; do not redesign.

Parchment prompt: Extract the original pale cream parchment; remove all wood and ornaments and extend paper edge to edge; retain subtle fine grain and soft darker edges; no motifs, flowers, clouds, text or frame remnants.
